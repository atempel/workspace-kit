## 2026-07-29 — Reading `queue/` and `SESSIONS.md` belongs to the inspection layer
- **Decision:** `core/inspect.js` gains `parseQueueItem()` and `parseSessions()`, exposing `index.queue` and `index.sessions`. Neither #80 nor #81 grows its own parser.
- **Reason:** cross-referencing the Claude Design prototype's 238 `{{ }}` placeholders against the specs exposed an ownership hole. Both docs/specs/document-ingestion-queue.md (→ #80) and docs/specs/cross-tool-session-log.md (→ #81) explicitly defer *reading* their own files to the inspection layer — "the kind of thing the Workspace inspection layer exists to eventually provide" — but #77's Non-Goals stopped at files, instructions and the graph, so nobody owned it. The dashboard (#169) needs both, and leaving it unowned is exactly the five-slightly-different-parsers outcome #77 exists to prevent.
- **Decision:** queue status is **normalised** to `pending`/`ingested` while the raw string is kept, and a test asserts the parser agrees with the documented `grep -l "Status: Pending" queue/*.md` convention. Two ways of answering "is this ingested" that can disagree would be worse than one.
- **Scope note:** this is the *read* half only. Both specs' generation half — emitting `SESSIONS.md`, the standing "every session" instruction, and the `queue/` stub convention into generated workspaces on all three surfaces — is still open. This repo dogfoods both conventions already, but `core/generator.js` does not yet produce them.
